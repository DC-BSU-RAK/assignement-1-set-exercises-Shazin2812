Thalassery Biryani

Thalassery Biryani is a fragrant and flavorful rice dish from Kerala, known for its unique blend of spices, short-grain rice (jeerakasala), and tender meat. It's a festive dish that brings together taste, aroma, and tradition.

Ingredients for the Thalassery Biryani

  • 2 cups jeerakasala rice (kaima rice)
  • 500g chicken, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, slit
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seeds
  • 1/4 cup chopped coriander leaves
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 1/4 cup curd (yogurt)
  • Ghee and oil for cooking
  • Salt to taste
  • Whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaf)
  • Cashews and raisins (optional for garnish)

Method of Thalassery Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the jeerakasala rice for 20 minutes. Cook with whole spices until soft but not mushy. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil and ghee in a large pan. Fry the sliced onions until golden and crispy. Remove half for garnish.
  3. In the remaining onions, add ginger-garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Add green chilies, chopped tomatoes, turmeric, garam masala, and fennel seeds. Cook until tomatoes soften.
  5. Add the chicken, salt, curd, mint, and coriander. Cook until the chicken is tender and gravy thickens.
  6. Layer the rice over the chicken masala. Top with fried onions, mint, and coriander. Drizzle ghee if desired.
  7. Cover tightly and dum cook on low flame for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Serve hot, garnished with fried cashews and raisins if using. Best enjoyed with raita or salad.
