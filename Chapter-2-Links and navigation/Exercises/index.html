Overview

PUBG is a multiplayer battle royale game where up to 100 players parachute onto an island, scavenge for weapons and gear, and fight to be the last person or team standing. It is known for its intense gameplay, tactical strategy, and immersive maps.

Game Info

Characters

PUBG doesn’t focus on a narrative with set characters, but players can customize avatars with skins, outfits, and gear. The game includes themed events and collaborations featuring unique characters or costumes.

Reviews

PUBG has received positive reviews for its innovation in the battle royale genre, competitive gameplay, and frequent updates. Find more reviews on Metacritic.
